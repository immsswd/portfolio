As the Managed Care division expanded, the company needed a website to better introduce its healthcare services, products, and solutions to corporate clients. At the time, there was no dedicated developer assigned to the project, and hiring additional resources would have increased both cost and delivery time.

Although my primary role was as a Data Analyst, I took the initiative to support the project and contributed to the development of the MQARE website using React.js and Next.js. Working closely with business stakeholders, I helped translate requirements into a functional and user-friendly web application that effectively represented the Managed Care business.

The website became a central platform for showcasing healthcare services, improving client engagement, and strengthening the company's digital presence. By taking ownership beyond my core responsibilities, I helped accelerate project delivery, reduced the need for additional development resources, and demonstrated the ability to adapt to new technologies when the business required it.

This experience reinforced an important principle in my career: solving business problems is often more valuable than staying within the boundaries of a job title. Whether through analytics, reporting, or software development, my focus remains on delivering solutions that create measurable business value.

**Technology:** React JS, Next JS
