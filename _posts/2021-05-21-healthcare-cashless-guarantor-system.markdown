As the company was in its early growth stage and healthcare operations were largely managed through manual processes, I designed and implemented a Cashless Management System to centralize and automate the end-to-end healthcare administration workflow.

The system serves as a core platform for the Cashless Team, enabling them to efficiently manage healthcare cases and monitor operational activities across the TPA process. Key capabilities include:

* Managing inpatient and outpatient healthcare cases
* Creating and maintaining patient admissions
* Generating initial and final guarantee letters
* Monitoring case progress and treatment status
* Recording operational activities and audit trails
* Calculating member copayments automatically
* Capturing and classifying diagnoses using ICD-10 standards
* Producing operational, financial, and management reports

By digitizing previously manual processes, the platform transformed healthcare operations into a centralized and data-driven workflow. The system significantly reduced administrative effort, minimized manual errors, and accelerated case processing and approval cycles, enabling faster service delivery to healthcare providers and members.

Beyond operational efficiency, the solution established a reliable data foundation for business analytics and reporting. Management gained real-time visibility into healthcare operations, utilization trends, case performance, and financial metrics, allowing stakeholders to make faster and more informed decisions. The platform also improved team productivity, enhanced monitoring capabilities, and provided the scalability needed to support the company's continued growth and increasing transaction volumes.
