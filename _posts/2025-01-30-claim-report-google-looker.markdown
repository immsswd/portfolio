As reimbursement activities continued to grow, the Claims Department relied heavily on manually compiled Excel files to track employee healthcare reimbursements. This process made it difficult to monitor claim performance, identify trends, and provide timely insights to management. Data was scattered across multiple spreadsheets, resulting in time-consuming reporting cycles and limited visibility into reimbursement activities.

To address this challenge, I designed and developed a centralized Claim Analytics Dashboard using Google Looker. The dashboard consolidates reimbursement data into a single source of truth, enabling stakeholders to monitor key metrics such as claim volume, reimbursement costs, inpatient and outpatient utilization, copayment amounts, and reimbursement trends across different periods.

By transforming raw operational data into interactive visual analytics, the solution significantly reduced manual reporting efforts and improved data accessibility across the organization. Management can now quickly identify spending patterns, monitor healthcare utilization, detect unusual claim trends, and evaluate cost performance without relying on multiple Excel reports.

The dashboard empowered the Claims Department and business leaders with real-time visibility into reimbursement operations, enabling faster, data-driven decision-making, improving reporting efficiency, and supporting more effective healthcare cost management across the organization.
